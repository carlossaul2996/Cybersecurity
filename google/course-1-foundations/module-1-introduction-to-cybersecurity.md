# Introduction to CyberSecurity 

- **Source:** Google CyberSecurity Course 1· Module: 1

## What I learned
Cybersecurity is the practice of protecting networks, devices, people, and data from threats such as unauthorized access and attacks. Security analysts, including SOC analysts, monitor systems, detect threats, and respond to incidents. Technical skills are also important in this role: analysts use SQL to query logs for signs of suspicious activity and programming to automate repetitive tasks.
## Tool
None, theory module.



## Real-world use
A SOC analyst receives a SIEM alert about many failed login attempts on one account in a few minutes, a possible brute force attack. The analyst reviews the logs to check the source, timing, and pattern, confirms whether it is malicious, then follows the incident response plan by locking the account and blocking the source IP address.
