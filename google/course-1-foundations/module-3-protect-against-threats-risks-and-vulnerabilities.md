# Module 3: Protect against threats, risks and vulnerabilities

- **Source:** Google Course 1 · Module 3

## What I learned
Security frameworks are guidelines for building plans that reduce risks to data and privacy, and security controls are safeguards that reduce specific risks. Security professionals use the CIA triad (confidentiality, integrity and availability) to choose the right controls, and controls work alongside frameworks to help organizations meet compliance, which means following internal standards and external regulations. Examples include the NIST Cybersecurity Framework (CSF), GDPR for E.U. residents' data, PCI DSS for credit card information and HIPAA for patient health information.

## Real-world use
An organization has a data breach that exposes the data of E.U. citizens. Under GDPR, it has 72 hours to inform the affected people, so security analysts need to know this regulation to help the organization stay compliant.
