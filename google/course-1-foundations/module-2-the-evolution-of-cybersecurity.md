# The evolution of cybersecurity

- **Source:** Google Course 1 · Module 2 


## What I learned
A computer virus is malicious code that disrupts how a computer works and damages data and software. Social engineering takes advantage of human error to get private information or access, with attacks like USB baiting and watering hole attacks. Phishing is a form of social engineering that uses digital communications to trick people, with attacks such as Business Email Compromise (BEC).

## Tool
None, theory module.


## Real-world use
An employee receives an email that seems to come from a known source, with a request for information that looks legitimate. A security analyst recognizes it as Business Email Compromise (BEC), an attack meant to get a financial advantage, and warns the employee not to reply.

## Open question
If a BEC email seems to come from a known source and the request looks legitimate, how can it be identified as fake before anyone replies?
